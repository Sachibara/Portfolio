PORTFOLIO EVIDENCE

What to capture from Packet Tracer

01

Topology screenshot

Show device names, links, VLAN/site labels, servers, and WAN relationships.

02

Control-plane evidence

Capture show ip ospf neighbor, show standby brief, EtherChannel, STP, and routing tables.

03

Security evidence

Capture ACL hit counters, NAT translations, SSH-only management, and successful/denied traffic tests.

04

Failure test

Shut a redundant link or gateway and demonstrate recovery without losing the intended service.

05

Engineering write-up

Explain design decisions, tradeoffs, verification, faults found, and corrective actions.

06

Final .pkt artifact

Save the implemented lab from Cisco Packet Tracer and add it beside the engineering brief in GitHub.
