System architecture

Built around the work,
not the hype.

The uploaded Zapier account export confirms an Interfaces form trigger, an AI qualification step, a Zapier Tables lookup/update flow, and four conditional paths. Path A routes hot leads to Slack, Path B creates an AI-assisted Gmail draft for warm leads, Path C updates cold lead records, and Path D handles human-review leads through email.

This browser simulation mirrors those categories. Its classification rules and sample messages are illustrative; the production credentials, prompt instructions, business identifiers, and original private export are never published.

What it demonstrates

A practical automation story.

Lead intake, prioritized routing, CRM-style record updates, notifications, assisted drafting, and human escalation—presented as a testable case study rather than an unverified claim that live integrations are active.
